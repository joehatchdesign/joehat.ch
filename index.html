About

I’m VP of Product at StatsDrone, the business-intelligence platform affiliate marketers use to track, reconcile and grow what they earn. I sit on the senior leadership team and run product, pricing and data architecture, and I created NousViz, our open-source data-intelligence platform.

I got here the technical way: twelve years from software engineer to the leadership table, across lead generation, affiliate publishing, enterprise e-learning, and the iGaming industry’s biggest events group. That covers most sides of the affiliate business, which is useful when you’re building software for it.

Alongside the day job I build AI agent systems, advise operators and affiliates on tracking and attribution, and speak at industry events about data transparency.
